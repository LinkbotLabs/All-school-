Page 60 · Unit 5 · Section A: Listening. This lesson is based on the New Frontiers 3A publisher transcript. Please check the activity layout and answers against your printed Thai-market book before teaching.

Lesson focus

Listening for food and meal vocabulary and understanding people expressing food preferences.

Suggested lesson length: 35–40 minutes.

Teaching sequence: Vocabulary listening → examples → pair speaking → listening comprehension.

Activity 1 · Vocabulary listening

Student instruction: Listen and number the words in your textbook.

1. Dessert
Sweet food eaten after a meal.
2. Breakfast
The first meal of the day.
3. Lunch
The meal eaten in the middle of the day.
4. Fast food
Food prepared and served quickly.
5. Side dish
A smaller dish served with the main food.
6. Dinner
The main meal eaten in the evening.
7. Main course
The most important dish in a meal.
8. Junk food
Food that is often high in fat, salt or sugar.

Activity 2 · Give examples

Student instruction: Write an example of each kind of food or meal in your book.

  1. Breakfast
  2. Lunch
  3. Dinner
  4. Junk food
  5. Fast food
  6. Main course
  7. Side dish
  8. Dessert

Teacher prompt: “What do you usually eat for breakfast?” Invite several students to share their examples.

Activity 3 · Speaking practice

Student instruction: Practise the preference questions and answers shown in your textbook with a partner.

Teacher focus: Model the pronunciation and natural stress in would rather and would prefer. Have students practise the printed examples, then swap roles.

Board reminder: “I would rather have …” / “I would prefer …”

Activity 4 · Listening comprehension

Student instruction: Listen and circle the correct answers in your textbook.

TEACHER TRANSCRIPT · TRACK 5-02 Number 1 W: Hey, Jake. Do you want to get breakfast with me? M: Sure. What are you having? W: I have a very busy morning, so I’m going to get fast food—maybe an egg sandwich or coffee and a scone. M: Hmm. I’d rather have the egg sandwich. I don’t really like sweets in the morning. W: OK. Let’s go. Number 2 M: Hi, Elizabeth. Are you busy this Friday night? W: No, why do you ask? M: Do you want to have dinner with me at Le Fantasie? W: Oh, that sounds great. Count me in! M: Great! Um… I have to get your order before we go. The main courses are beef bourguignon or duck à l’orange. W: Hard choice. I would rather have the beef bourguignon than the duck. M: Would you like the French onion soup with that or a salad? W: I really like French onion soup, but I think I prefer to have the salad. M: OK! See you Friday. W: See you, Ben!

Answer check from the publisher Teacher's Guide material previously consulted: Number 1 – egg sandwich. Number 2 – beef bourguignon and salad (confirm the exact response format in your book).

Quick review

  • Can students identify the eight food and meal words?
  • Can students give an example of each category?
  • Can students express a preference using “would rather” or “would prefer”?
  • Can students identify the food choices in the two listening conversations?
